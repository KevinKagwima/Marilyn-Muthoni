A little about me

{% if profile and profile.biography %} Interrogating power. Reimagining what's possible. {% else %} Interrogating power. Reimagining what's possible. {% endif %}

Trained as a corporate finance lawyer, I founded one of Kenya's first commercial records and information management companies — grew it to profitability, and sold it to an international corporation in 2018.

Today I work at the intersection of law, business, and politics — as a consultant on strategy, gender, and policy, as a writer on the constitutional and political questions that shape women's lives, and as an advocate whose organizing spans #WeAre52pc to #EndFemicide.

J.D. & LL.M., International and Comparative Law — Cornell Law School
B.A., Political Science — Northeastern University
Vital Voices GROW Fellow — 2013–14
