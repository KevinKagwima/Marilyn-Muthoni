About the book

A decade of constitutional violations - and a case for why they cannot stand.

In this collection of articles written over a decade - from 2016 to 2026 - Marilyn documents the violations of Article 27(8) of the Bill of Rights by, among others, the Supreme Court, Parliament, and the Executive. In addition to providing an important record of the unconstitutionality perpetrated by institutions of the State, Marilyn interrogates the basis of these decisions and questions the normalised state of unconstitutionality in Kenya today.

Marilyn makes important arguments about the non-negotiability of women citizens to Kenya's constitutional democracy post-2010.

The central argument

At the core of her argument is that women are sovereign - and that all branches of government (the executive, parliament, and the judiciary) lack the constitutional authority to derogate from this sovereignty.
